How I work

Plain language, no surprises. You don’t need to know any jargon; I’ll explain each decision and why it matters for your visitors.

  1. Listen

    We talk through what your business or organization does, who your visitors are, and what you need the site to accomplish. I’ll give you a clear scope and a fixed price.

  2. Build

    I write clean, fast pages with accessibility built in from the first line, not bolted on at the end. You’ll see progress at every step and can review on your own phone or computer.

  3. Test

    I test every page with VoiceOver, JAWS, and NVDA, with keyboard only, at 200% zoom, and in both light and dark mode, then run automated checks and a security review before launch.

  4. Launch and support

    Your site goes live on fast, secure hosting with HTTPS, security headers, and automatic deploys from GitHub. I hand over the keys and stay available for updates.

Accessibility statement

Accessibility is the whole point of this business, so this site is held to the same standard I hold client work to.

What this site does

  • Meets the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.
  • Tested with VoiceOver on macOS and iOS, and with JAWS and NVDA on Windows.
  • Works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator on every control.
  • Follows your device’s light or dark setting, with a manual override in the header, and keeps text contrast at 4.5:1 or better in both.
  • Uses the Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, designed for low-vision readers, and stays readable at 200% zoom and in a single column on small screens.
  • Respects your reduced-motion setting; there are no animations that can’t be turned off.
  • Works without JavaScript: the contact form still sends, and the theme still follows your device.

Tell me if something isn’t working

If any part of this site is hard to use with your assistive technology, I want to know. Email dwayne@abvtech.net and describe what you were trying to do and what happened. I aim to reply within two business days and fix confirmed problems promptly.
